> *There is no story that is not true... The world has no end, and what is good among one people is an abomination with others.*
> Chinua Achebe, *Things Fall Apart*

Son of Man, hewn of a rough sheepskin,
Filled with tepid primordial swamp,
What beast are you, time come round at last,
Who slouches forth to arid farmland,

What ambrosia could quench,
The thirst set on your father's fruit,
Insatiable lust for cultivating, tilling,
and irrigating fields with brother's blood?
